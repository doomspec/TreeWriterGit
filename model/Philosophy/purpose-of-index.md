# Purpose of INDEX.md

`INDEX.md` enables two ways of writing: top down and bottom up.

In top-down writing, a human writes outlines, story, and summary in `INDEX.md`, and AI expands it into lower-level Markdown files and folders.

In bottom-up writing, a human writes Markdown files, and AI creates or refreshes `INDEX.md` with a summary of the children.

Each `INDEX.md` should have a frontmatter field saying at which commit it was composed so it can be regenerated when its children change.
