# The Accessibility Gap

## Purpose

Explain why technological accessibility—not only biological complexity—is limiting progress.

## Key message

Biofabrication technologies exist, but they are difficult to access, integrate, and reproduce.

## Notes

* Pivot with **However...** after the opportunity paragraph.

* Name the enabling stack: bioprinting, microfabrication, microfluidics, automated liquid handling, imaging, sensing, computational tools.

* Barriers: cost, proprietary ecosystems, specialized manufacturing, integration expertise.

* Close on fragmentation → reproducibility, innovation speed, and uneven participation.

* **open** → accessible designs and protocols

* **interoperable** → technologies work together

* **reusable** → workflows can be adopted and adapted by other laboratories
