# Here We Argue: DIY Democratizes the Stack

## Purpose

Introduce the central thesis of the Perspective.

## Key message

DIY biofabrication is about democratizing technology through openness, modularity, and interoperability—not merely reducing cost.

## Notes

- Use **Here, we argue...** as the explicit pivot.
- Emphasize open-source hardware, accessible fabrication, interoperable software, modular automation, community protocols.
- Beyond cost: transparency, rapid prototyping, reproducibility, collaborative innovation.
- Do not preview the three tool categories here—that belongs in Section 2.
