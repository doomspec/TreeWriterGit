# The Ability To Engineer Complex Tissues Is Increasing

Overview:
- **Claim:** Tissue engineering has become a central goal of biomedical research with heavy demand for human-relevant tissue models across regenerative medicine, disease modeling, and drug discovery.
  - **Evidence/Citations:** [@hofer_engineering_2021; @ingber_human_2022; @low_organs-on-chips_2021]
- **Claim:** Recent advances in biofabrication introduce powerful capabilities for constructing multi-material, architecturally complex, and functional tissues.
  - **Evidence/Citations:** [@daly_bioprinting_2021; @levato_volumetric_2025; @cooke_biofabrication_2025; @huang_organoid_2025; @editorial_pushing_2025]
- **Claim:** Recapitulating native organ complexity, organization, and function remains difficult, and increasingly depends on the integration of complementary biofabrication, microfluidic platforms, and automated liquid handling.
  - **Evidence/Citations:** [@he_high-resolution_2024; @editorial_pushing_2025; @zhao_integrating_2024; @dettinger_open-source_2022]
