# Why Complex Tissue Engineering Matters

## Purpose

Establish the importance of complex tissue engineering and explain why advanced biofabrication is becoming increasingly important.

## Key message

Engineering functional human tissues is becoming central to modern biomedical research. The field now requires not only biological advances but also the integration of complementary engineering technologies to recreate tissue architecture, physiology, and function.
