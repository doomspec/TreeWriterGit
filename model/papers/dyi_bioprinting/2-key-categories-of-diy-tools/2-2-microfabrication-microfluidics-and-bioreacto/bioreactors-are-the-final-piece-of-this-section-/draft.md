Bioreactors are the final piece of this section as they convert fabricated structures into living tissues through controlled culture conditions, mechanical stimulation, and perfusion. The bIUreactor is one of the open-source 3D tissue research platforms that allows researchers to build perfusable tissues, apply cyclic compression, and culture scaffold-free constructs in their own laboratories for a fraction of the cost of proprietary systems. Its design explicitly aims to democratize 3D tissue research by replacing proprietary complexity with a reproducible, open hardware stack. Similarly, the Build Your Own Bioreactor (BYOB) bioreactor design demonstrates that open-source perfusion systems can be shared and customized for tissue culture workflows, which is highlighting the idea that democratization also includes the culture environment, not only the fabrication step.
