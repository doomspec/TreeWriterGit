# Democratizing Tissue Engineering: Alleviating the Technological Barriers to Advanced Biofabrication

DIY biofabrication democratizes the tissue-engineering technology stack through openness, modularity, and interoperability—not merely lower cost.

## Why this topic matters

Engineering increasingly complex human tissues is essential for regenerative medicine, disease modelling, drug discovery, and the transition toward human-relevant experimental models. Although biological understanding continues to advance, translating this knowledge into reproducible tissue manufacturing requires integrating multiple biofabrication technologies. Access to these technologies remains uneven, creating a growing gap between what is scientifically possible and what most laboratories can practically achieve.

## Audience

* Tissue engineers and biofabrication researchers
* Organ-on-chip and organoid developers
* Robotics and laboratory automation researchers
* Open-source hardware and software communities
* Academic core facilities and translational laboratories
* Early-career investigators seeking accessible biofabrication platforms
* Funding agencies and research infrastructure developers

## Narrative arc (Reinschrift)

* **Why?** Human tissue engineering is increasingly important for biomedical research and medicine.
* **However...** Advanced biofabrication remains fragmented and inaccessible.
* **Here, we argue...** DIY biofabrication democratizes the technology stack through openness and interoperability.
* **So what?** Open, AI-enabled biofabrication ecosystems can accelerate innovation and broaden participation.

**The limiting factor is no longer access to individual technologies but access to the multidisciplinary knowledge required to integrate them into complete biofabrication workflows.**
